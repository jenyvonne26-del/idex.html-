📘 Mean（Mean）

Everyone’s salary affects the mean. An extremely high salary usually pulls the mean upward.

For 120 employees, the median is the average of the 60th and 61st salaries. A single extreme value does not necessarily change the median.

📐 Pearson Correlation coefficient

r = Σ[(x − x̄)(y − ȳ)] ÷ √[Σ(x − x̄)² Σ(y − ȳ)²]

x is tenure and y is monthly salary. r ranges from −1 to +1 and describes the direction and strength of the linear relationship.

🧭 How do you interpret r?

Near +1:Positive linear relationship.
Near −1:Negative linear relationship.
Near 0:Weak linear relationship.

Interpretation should also consider context, distribution, and sample characteristics.
